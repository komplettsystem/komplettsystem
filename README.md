Other work, including a model for workspaces shared by people and agents, is private

## How I work

- **Understand the customer's problem before the solution.**
- **Never ask customers what we can answer ourselves.** Their time goes to the value, not the setup.
- **Test an idea from every side before engineering commits:** feasibility, user value, whether it removes work for the people operating it, and adoption.
- **Know the limits of a technology, standard or partner before choosing it:** build, buy or partner, layer by layer.
